Privacy policy

The short version

This site has no server or database of its own. Everything it keeps is kept in your browser, on your device. Your browser talks directly to Spotify; nothing is sent to the site’s owner or to anyone else. The site sets no cookies and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What is kept in your browser

  • Your Spotify login: the access and refresh tokens Spotify gives the site, and the permissions you allowed.
  • Your Spotify user ID, so that the libraries of two people who use the same browser are kept apart.
  • Your library: Spotify song IDs, the dances, tags and corrections you gave them, the songs you removed, and the ID of your library playlist; and when you last downloaded a backup.
  • Song details from Spotify (titles, artists, albums, lengths, recording codes, release years, whether a song can play in your country), kept for at most 14 days, then read again.
  • Your settings, your own dances and your keyword tag rules.
  • For the live view and the program: the order of the night’s songs (Spotify IDs and dances), and the songs you matched or added during the night (Spotify IDs and dances).
  • A Spotify key you pasted, if you use your own; for the site’s owner only, the folder chosen for publishing the shared library.
  • That you agreed to these terms, and when.

All of this uses your browser’s own storage for websites (“local storage”, “session storage” and “IndexedDB”), which other websites cannot read. Files you download (library backups, settings files, the program) are saved on your computer and are yours.

What is sent where

Your browser connects directly to Spotify (accounts.spotify.com to log in, api.spotify.com for everything else) to: log in; read your Spotify user ID; list your playlists and read the ones you choose to import; search for songs; create and keep your private library playlist up to date; create the playlists you save; and, in the live view, see what is playing. What Spotify does with this is covered by Spotify’s own privacy policy. Nothing goes to the site’s owner or to anyone else.

The permissions the site asks Spotify for: read your private playlists and the ones you collaborate on; create and change playlists (private, or public if you tick it); and see what is playing (the live view, read only).

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, which delivers the site’s files to your browser, may record technical information such as your IP address; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies, and lets no other company place cookies to track your browsing. One exception: the library builder’s song preview (▶) shows Spotify’s own player, loaded from open.spotify.com, which may set Spotify’s cookies under Spotify’s policies. It loads only when you press ▶.

To manage cookies, use your browser’s settings: you can block or delete cookies for open.spotify.com (the preview may then not play) or for any site.

How long, and deleting it

Everything stays in your browser until you delete it, except Spotify’s song details (at most 14 days). Logging out removes your Spotify login and the song details. Delete everything this site keeps in this browser… (in the Spotify connection panel on the generator and library builder pages) removes all of it at once; download a backup first if you want to keep your library. Clearing this site’s data in your browser’s settings does the same. To remove the site’s access to your Spotify account, use Spotify’s Manage apps page.
